Passport

The Moeezland Passport is an official travel and diplomatic document issued exclusively to citizens of the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland. Moeezland operates a single unified passport system, meaning there are no separate passport categories such as ordinary, diplomatic, or official passports. Every passport holder is issued the same passport and enjoys the same rights and responsibilities under Moeezland law.

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must satisfy all of the following requirements before a passport may be issued: Be a recognised citizen of the Constitutional Emirate of Moeezland, Obtain authorisation from the Government of Moeezland or the Office of the Emir, Successfully apply for and receive Diplomatic Protection Status before submitting a passport application. Agree that, when required, the Government of Moeezland or the Office of the Emir may temporarily appoint the passport holder as an official delegate or representative of Moeezland at intermicronational meetings, conferences, diplomatic missions, or other official events.
